IMPLEMENT A STRUCTURAL WALLACE TREE FOR THE CMAC MULTIPLIER

The CMAC multiplier's reduction stage uses a generic component that must be
replaced with a structural Wallace tree. Study the source to understand the
reduction topology and operating modes. Replace the existing generic component
instances in the multiplier source with your structural implementation.
